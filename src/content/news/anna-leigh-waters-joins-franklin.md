**Anna Leigh Waters'** move from **Paddletek** to **Franklin** reshapes the top of the women's sponsorship board. Industry coverage in early 2026 described the deal as long-term and one of the year's biggest sponsor shifts. Franklin has said it plans to build signature gear around Waters, including paddles in its **C45** carbon-fiber line.

## What sponsorship moves mean for club shoppers

Pro endorsements change catalog calendars and ad creative faster than they change how a paddle feels in your hand. Expect more Franklin signature shapes, co-branded accessories, and Waters-facing product photography to show up through 2026 as the partnership ramps. That does **not** mean every Franklin SKU suddenly plays like Waters' tournament setup, and it does not make prior Paddletek paddles “obsolete.”

A useful rule: treat signature lines as marketing + model identity, then evaluate the paddle on the same criteria you would use without a pro name attached — weight, balance, grip, thickness, and face.
