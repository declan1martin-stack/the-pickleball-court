## National Championship heads to the GTA

Pickleball Canada's 2026 National Championship runs August 25–30 at the Pickleplex Social Club's Downsview and Vaughan locations in the Greater Toronto Area — 21 indoor courts hosting singles, doubles, and mixed doubles, plus the Provincial Team Competition. That competition now doubles as an evaluation stage for Pickleball Canada's National Development Program, so this year's event carries a bit more weight than usual for players hoping to move up the pathway.

## The numbers keep climbing

Pickleball Canada's annual participation survey puts the country at roughly 1.80 million players in 2026, up from 1.54 million in 2025 — about 14% growth in a single year. Paid membership grew too, up 10% to 93,888. Ontario leads participation, followed by Quebec and British Columbia, which tracks with population distribution more than anything sport-specific. For context: the same survey had Canada at just 1 million players back in 2022, so this is roughly 80% growth over four years. However you slice it, this isn't a market that's plateauing.

## CNPL's fourth season brought the world to Oakville

The Canadian National Pickleball League launched its fourth season on May 9 in Oakville, and this year it partnered with the Association of Pickleball Players (APP) to form the Global Pro Pickleball Tour — bringing internationally ranked players from four continents into the same league structure for the first time. It's a meaningful step up in competition depth for a league that's still only four years old.
