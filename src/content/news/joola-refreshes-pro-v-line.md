If you are shopping the top of JOOLA's range, check which Pro V generation a listing actually ships. Frame and core language changes between model years, and “Pro V” alone is no longer enough to know what you are buying.

JOOLA's **2026 Pro V** update — part of **Ben Johns'** signature series — adds **KineticFrame** technology that brand materials describe as flexing and storing energy on contact, then releasing it into the ball. That framing keeps JOOLA in the premium all-court conversation even as foam-core competitors push their own durability and feel claims. We are summarizing the brand's stated design intent here; we are not claiming independent lab verification of energy return percentages or similar marketing metrics.

Premium lines often leave older generations in marketplaces for months. Before you click buy:

- Confirm thickness and shape against the job you want (all-court vs. power-first).

## Foam competition does not make Pro V obsolete

Foam-core launches get the headlines in 2026, but polymer-core and hybrid constructions still dominate many recreational and intermediate bags. A Pro V refresh is JOOLA's answer to staying relevant at the top of the price ladder — not proof that every player needs the newest signature paddle. Buy the generation that matches your touch and budget, and ignore year-over-year FOMO unless your current paddle is clearly holding you back.
