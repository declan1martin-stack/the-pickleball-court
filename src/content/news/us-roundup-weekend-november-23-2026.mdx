Championship Sunday closed the [Proton Daytona Beach Open](https://www.ppatour.com/events/2026/proton-daytona-beach-open/) at Pictona at Holly Hill. Augie Ge and JW Johnson walked out with the men’s doubles trophy for the second year in a row. Anna Leigh Waters left with two golds from two draws. Judit Castillo took women’s singles. Hunter Johnson took men’s singles. Five finals, five sweeps. Official board: the [PPA Daytona hub](https://www.ppatour.com/events/2026/proton-daytona-beach-open/).

This is not last week’s [Mesa APP recap](/news/us-roundup-weekend-november-16-2026). The APP’s pro tour was dark. The PPA was not. Mesa closed Nov. 15. Daytona closed Nov. 22. It is a 1,000-point Open, the last of that size on the 2026 PPA calendar, with a listed payout of $1,063,327. Finals were best-of-three.

Waters sat singles and still left as the only double gold medalist on the grounds. Ben Johns sat men’s doubles and played only mixed. Jack Munro and Megan Fudge were not in this draw. Their APP numbers — seven straight men’s doubles golds, 33 women’s golds — did not move because they were not here.

## Saturday: ten semifinals, ten sweeps

Saturday at Pictona ran five divisions and exactly 20 games. Nobody needed a third. Federico Staksrud and Zane Ford came closest. Andrei Daescu and Christian Alshon against Riley Newman and Noe Khlif were next. Every player who won game one won the match. Scores confirmed against [pickleball.com](https://pickleball.com/news/ten-semifinals-ten-sweeps-at-the-daytona-beach-open) and the [PPA draws](https://www.ppatour.com/events/2026/proton-daytona-beach-open/).

**Men’s singles semis.** No. 2 Hunter Johnson ended John Lucian Goins’ bid for a third straight domestic title, 11-7, 11-7. Goins arrived from Virginia Beach and Worlds without a dropped singles game in either draw. He dropped two here. No. 3 Staksrud beat No. 8 Ford 11-8, 13-11. Staksrud had already taken out JW Johnson in the quarters.

**Women’s singles semis.** No. 3 Castillo took the opener 11-1 against top seed Brooke Buckner and closed 11-8. She was the only lower seed to win a semifinal all day. No. 2 Kiora Kunimoto beat defending Daytona champion Catherine Parenteau 11-7, 11-8.

**Mixed semis.** All four top seeds made the final four, and neither match was close. No. 1 Waters and Johns held defending Daytona mixed champions Jorja Johnson and JW Johnson to eight points across two games, 11-3, 11-5. No. 2 Anna Bright and Alshon beat Tyra Hurricane Black and Eric Oncins 11-4, 11-1.

**Men’s doubles semis.** No. 2 Ge and JW Johnson ended the week of No. 13 Will Howells and AJ Koller, 11-7, 11-5. Howells and Koller had already taken out No. 4 Staksrud and Pablo Tellez. No. 1 Daescu and Alshon edged last year’s Daytona finalists, Newman and Khlif, 12-10, 11-9.

**Women’s doubles semis.** No. 1 Bright and Waters beat No. 6 Mari Humberg and Milan Rane 11-4, 11-1. Humberg and Rane had rallied from a game down on Friday to beat Worlds finalists Sofia Sewing and Parenteau. No. 2 Black and Jorja Johnson beat No. 3 Rachel Rohrabacher and Parris Todd 11-5, 11-3.

Saturday set No. 1 vs. No. 2 in all three doubles finals. Bright and Waters would be on opposite sides of the net in mixed, then teammates in women’s doubles. Daytona’s finals were best-of-three. The question for Sunday was who would force a third.

Sunday added five more sweeps. Ten games. No decider. Combined with Saturday, that is 15 consecutive semifinal and championship matches without a third game. [The Dink](https://www.thedinkpickleball.com/ppa-daytona-beach-open-recap-castillo-breaks-through-waters-doubles-up-and-championship-sunday-delivers-five-sweeps/) puts the last time all five gold-medal matches ended in the minimum number of games on the domestic PPA Tour at the PPA Finals on May 10.

**Mixed opened it.** Bright and Alshon reunited for the first time since 2024 and earned another shot at the pair that beat them for gold that year. Waters and Johns took the rematch 11-4, 11-8. Johns’ only draw of the week. Waters’ first of two.

**Castillo then closed women’s singles 12-10, 11-2.** A day after taking out Buckner, she edged Kunimoto in the opener and pulled away in the second. It adds a Daytona title to the singles gold she won at the 2024 Austin Open, and it follows a PPA Tour Europe Barcelona 250 triple crown in September. Kunimoto is still looking for a first PPA singles gold. She is getting closer.

**Women’s doubles was 11-2, 11-4.** Bright and Waters went from opponents in the mixed final to teammates in the women’s final. They won their 19th consecutive tournament together. They have not lost a match as a pair in 2026. Waters’ second gold in the two events she entered.

![Two men’s doubles partners celebrating a championship point on an outdoor Florida hard court](/images/news/weekend-recap-daytona-mens-doubles-repeat-2026.jpg)

*Ge and JW Johnson defended the Daytona men’s doubles title they won in five games a year ago. Illustration.*

**Hunter Johnson closed men’s singles 11-8, 11-7.** After ending Goins on Saturday, he beat Staksrud in straight games and did not drop a game in his last two matches. Daytona is his fifth singles gold of the season after Cary, Las Vegas, Australia, and Hong Kong. Staksrud had been the cleaner path: JW Johnson in the quarters, Ford in a 13-11 second game. The final was not a third.

**Men’s doubles was the match the grounds had billed as an upset.** Daescu and Alshon came in as the No. 1 seeds. Ge, ranked 19th, and JW Johnson, ranked fifth, beat them 11-8, 11-3. Same venue as 2025, when the pair beat Newman and Khlif 11-8 in a fifth game that remains the longest match in Carvana PPA Tour history. This year the format was best-of-three. The result was the same gold. Two weeks ago at Worlds, JW Johnson and Tama Shimabukuro came back from 0-2 against this same Daescu/Alshon pair. Different partner. Same ending.

## Around the grounds

Howells and Koller’s semifinal run as the No. 13 seeds was the week’s loudest men’s doubles story that did not end in gold. Their round-of-16 win over Staksrud and Tellez, 11-6, 11-9, is why Saturday’s board did not look like the seed list.

**MLP Asia’s Saturday night in Ho Chi Minh City did happen.** The Viet Panthers hosted at AP Sports Club 2. They beat Malayan Stripes 3-2 on a 21-12 DreamBreaker, then swept THE DOTS TOKYO 4-0 and handed the league leaders their first loss of the season. Shimabukuro, two weeks removed from a Worlds men’s doubles gold, won all four of his games and closed the DreamBreaker. Official write-up: [MLP Asia](https://mlp-asia.com/panthers-defend-the-den-after-all-feline-fight-with-stripes/). The Panthers sit first on five points from two matches, level with THE DOTS TOKYO, who have played three. Manila’s Friday night — Dragon Warriors 3-1 over Helios, Hong Kong Cannons 3-1 over Helios — sits outside this recap window. Do not recap it as Saturday’s result.

**The APP Selkirk U.S. Collegiate Series Washington, D.C. Regional** ran Saturday and Sunday at Down the Line Sports Center in Annandale, Virginia, the second of eight regionals on the road to Las Vegas in March. The APP had not posted a confirmed champion by our deadline. We will not invent one. Event hub: [APP DC Regional](https://www.theapp.global/tour-schedule/2026-27-app-selkirk-us-collegiate-series-washington).

## What this weekend means

1. **Daytona’s 1,000-point board closed without a third game on Championship Sunday.** Five sweeps after ten Saturday sweeps is not a fluke format footnote. Best-of-three finals at an Open this deep will keep producing short gold-medal matches until someone forces the extra game.
2. **Waters can skip singles and still leave with two titles.** Mixed with Johns, women’s doubles with Bright. Nineteen straight as a pair. Undefeated together in 2026. The singles field she sat is the one Castillo just won.
3. **JW Johnson is the men’s doubles constant.** Worlds with Shimabukuro from 0-2. Daytona with Ge, back-to-back at the same club, against the same top seeds he beat in Dallas. Ge is still not a weekly PPA partner. Pictona is still their place.
4. **Hunter Johnson is a five-title singles season.** Cary, Las Vegas, Australia, Hong Kong, Daytona. Goins’ two-draw run without a dropped game is over. The men’s singles hierarchy that left Dallas with Goins over Sock is not the hierarchy that left Holly Hill.
5. **MLP Asia has a table now.** Viet Panthers 2-0. THE DOTS TOKYO have a loss. The rest of the regular season is Kuala Lumpur. Hong Kong’s home night is still not in Hong Kong.
