Championship Sunday closed the APP Arizona Open at Arizona Athletic Grounds in Mesa. Alvi Regalado, the 41st and last seed in a 41-man draw, won men’s singles in his first APP Tour event. Andre Millet and Patrick Kawka ended Jack Munro and Richard Livornese Jr.’s seven-title men’s doubles run. Official board: the [APP Arizona recap](https://www.theapp.global/news/arizona-2026-recap).

This is not last month’s [MLP finals and Cary Nationals roundup](/news/us-roundup-mlp-finals-ppa-nationals-september-1-2026). It is not the Worlds triple crown at Brookhaven. The PPA Tour was dark in North America. The APP was not. Worlds closed Nov. 8. Mesa closed Nov. 15.

Two numbers arrived in Arizona and left unchanged in the way nobody wanted. Munro and Livornese still have seven straight men’s doubles golds — they do not have eight. Megan Fudge is still tied with Simone Jardim at 33 career women’s pro golds. The record goes to Fort Lauderdale.

## Saturday: the streak snaps, and the women’s draw keeps flipping

Men’s doubles was the match the APP had billed all week. Munro and Livornese took the first game of the final 11-3. Millet and Kawka, the No. 6 seeds in a first gold-medal match together, won the next two 11-3, 11-5. First career golds for both. The run stops at seven.

Jay Devilliers and Tanner Tomassi took bronze.

![Two men’s doubles partners celebrating an upset championship point on an outdoor desert hard court](/images/news/weekend-recap-mesa-mens-doubles-upset-2026.jpg)

*Mesa men’s doubles ended the season’s most reliable partnership. Illustration.*

Women’s doubles was no kinder to the chalk. No. 7 Vivian Glozman and Roos Van Reek beat No. 5 Katerina Stewart and Brooke Revuelta 11-6, 11-8. Second women’s doubles title of the season for the pair after Chicago. Arielle Butler and Allison Harris took bronze. Fudge and Jada Bui were already out, 11-2, 11-6 to Glozman and Van Reek in the quarterfinals. The No. 1 seeds, Christine Maddox and Amanda Hendry, finished fourth.

That is why Fudge’s record did not move. Mixed was gone on Friday, 11-1, 11-0 to Devilliers and Domenika Turkovic in the semi. Women’s doubles went in the quarters. Mesa will not give her the 34th.

Arizona State and Grand Canyon opened Sunday with a collegiate showcase. Then the pros.

**Mixed first.** Devilliers and Turkovic, the No. 12 seeds, had not dropped a game all week. They beat Glozman and Ronan Camron 11-2, 11-9. Glozman and Camron led 8-2 in the second game and still lost it. Devilliers’ first APP gold since 2022. Turkovic, an Arizona State tennis alum, went home with mixed gold on a second-straight APP weekend of two finals. Fudge and Aidan Schenk took bronze, 11-6, 11-6 over Emilia Schmidt and Daniel De la Rosa. One more medal for the tour’s all-time career medals leader. Not the one she came for.

**Regalado then closed men’s singles.** Path: Zack Marceau, Eduardo Irizarry, John Cangelosi, top seed Camron, fourth seed Ryler DeHeart, then Millet, who had just won doubles. Six matches. He took the first game of the final 11-7. Millet evened it 11-4. Regalado went 5-0 in the decider and won 11-5. Max Green took bronze. Asked what the difference was, Regalado said he wished he could tell you. A 41st seed in a first APP event does not need a better answer.

**Stewart took the rematch.** She and Turkovic had met in four APP singles finals this season. Turkovic won Louisville. Stewart won Mesa 11-4, 11-0, her fifth women’s singles gold of 2026. She now leads the series 3-1. Van Reek took bronze.

Add the seeds of the five pro champions and you get 67. Only Stewart was the player the draw said she would be.

## Around the grounds

The Pickleball Hall of Fame Class of 2026 was celebrated in Mesa: Matt Wright, Lucy Kovalova, and Dave Weinbach as competitors; Marsha Freso and Linda Laymon as contributors. Weinbach later took 50+ men’s doubles bronze the long way, losing 11-1, 11-3 and winning a medal off the same pair the same day.

AARP Champions (50+) on Sunday: Youssef Bouzidi and Dayne Gingrich won men’s doubles. Carolina Lopez Ascarate and Lee Whitwell won women’s doubles. Bouzidi and Lopez Ascarate each left with two golds for the week. Masters (60+) had already closed: Heather Iffert and Mark Wilder two golds each, including mixed together.

**PPA Tour Europe’s P125 in Lille** closed at JOCO Pickleball in Villeneuve-d’Ascq. Saturday doubles: James Ling and Martin Thomas beat Alberto Seccia and Davide Vendrame 11-3, 11-6. Karolina Owczarek and Caroline Nothnagel beat Myriam Enmer and Raquel Amaro 11-3, 11-1. Sunday mixed: Owczarek and Bartosz Karbownik came from a game down to beat Klara Thell-Lenntorp and Hector Sanchez Vidal 10-12, 11-2, 11-6. Two titles each for Owczarek and Karbownik. Friday singles — Karbownik over Théo Platel 11-8, 11-5; Manon Garcia over Viktória Kanichová 11-7, 11-4 — set that table. They are not this weekend’s North American story. They are why the [World Pickleball Rankings](/news/feature-global-game-august-21-2026) exist.

**MLP Asia’s Hong Kong night did not happen.** The Cannons were to host the Viet Panthers and Dragon Warriors at Discovery Bay Recreation Club on Saturday. MLP Asia postponed less than 30 minutes before first serve after the visa agency it used, Light Years Production, failed to process visiting work visas. Ticket holders get refunds. THE DOTS TOKYO opened Friday in Ikebukuro; that opener sits outside this recap window. Do not recap it as Saturday’s result. The next Asia dates are Manila on Nov. 20 and Ho Chi Minh City on Nov. 21.

Do not recap Worlds, the Nations Cup, or College Worlds as this weekend’s news. Those closed Nov. 8 and Nov. 1.

## What this weekend means

1. **The APP field is deeper than its seeds.** A last-seed singles champion and four of five golds off the top line is not a fluke draw. It is a tour that can no longer assume the favourite holds.
2. **Munro and Livornese are human.** Seven is still the number. Eight is not. Millet and Kawka are now the pair everyone else gets to scout.
3. **Fudge’s 34th waits.** Mixed and women’s doubles both ended before Sunday. The APP Tour Championships at The Fort in Fort Lauderdale, Dec. 3–6, are the next chance.
4. **Europe is on the same board.** Lille is a 125. Two titles for a Pole who arrived with 50 singles points still move the global list. Catherine Parenteau sits fifth on the PPA women’s list published Nov. 14. That list did not play this weekend. Daytona will.
5. **MLP Asia’s calendar just got a hole.** Two pool matches and Hong Kong’s only home night are looking for a date. Tama Shimabukuro’s Viet Panthers still have not played.
