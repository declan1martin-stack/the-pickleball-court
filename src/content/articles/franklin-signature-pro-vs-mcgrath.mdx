Get the Signature Pro if you compete in tournaments, because it is the advanced paddle of the two. Get the McGrath if you play recreationally, because it is the intermediate paddle and sits in the lower price band.

## Pick by how you play

- **Pop and power:** the Signature Pro, with its thinner 13mm core.
- **Control and a softer feel:** the McGrath, with its thicker 16mm core.
- **Reach:** the Signature Pro, with its elongated shape.
