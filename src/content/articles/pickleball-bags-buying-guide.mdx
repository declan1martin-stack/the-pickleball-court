## Which style fits you?

- **Sling bags** — minimalist, one paddle plus balls and a water bottle. Good for players who show up, play, and leave without a locker-room routine.
- **Backpacks** — the middle ground: multiple paddles, a change of shoes, apparel, and accessories, with better back support for carrying it all.
- **Tour bags** — built for players who travel to leagues or tournaments and need dedicated paddle compartments, insulated pockets, and real capacity.

## Buying tips

- **Check for padded paddle compartments** — extreme heat or cold in a trunk can affect paddle materials over time, especially carbon fiber faces.
- **Think about what you actually carry**, not what you might carry someday. A tour bag is overkill for someone who plays casually twice a month.
- **Ventilated shoe compartments** matter more than people expect — nobody wants their whole bag smelling like a gym shoe by day two of a tournament.
