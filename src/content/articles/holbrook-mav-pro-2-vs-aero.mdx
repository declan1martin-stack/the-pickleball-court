## Buying checklist

1. Name the miss in plain language: power/control hybrid with a printed 16mm core, or edgeless hand speed with a foam core.
2. Require the exact catalog sentence. T700 3K raw carbon, enhanced grit, 16mm honeycomb, and a comfort grip are the Mav Pro 2.0. Edgeless T700, foam polypropylene, corner weighting, and kitchen hand speed are the Aero.
3. Read the band, then Amazon.com. Both Holbrooks are **$$$$$.** Prism Flash is the anchor on this page that sits at **$$$$.** A value-price sentence on a Vatic listing does not rewrite a badge.
4. Read thickness only where it is printed. The Mav, Ruby, Prism Flash, Vatic Flash, and Perseus 16mm print 16mm. The Aero, CRBN Power, and Double Black Diamond Control do not. Confirm the cart on every SKU.
5. Keep raw carbon on the listings that print it: Mav Pro 2.0, Double Black Diamond Control, Prism Flash, and Vatic Flash. The Aero prints T700 carbon without the word raw. Ruby’s description says carbon-face performance and does not print raw or T700.
6. Keep thermoformed on the sentence that listing uses. The Mav uses it in the description. Ruby and Perseus 16mm print it on a construction bullet. The Aero, CRBN Power, Double Black Diamond, and both Vatic paddles on this page do not print the word.
7. Leave ounces, face inches, swing weight, and spin RPM blank. None of the Holbrook bullets include them. Lighter, wider, and manageable weight are not measurements.
8. Approval: both Holbrooks omit it. Vatic Flash prints USAPA approved. Perseus 16mm prints a USAPA-approved shape. For a sanctioned match, confirm the exact model on the [USA Pickleball approved equipment list](https://equipment.usapickleball.org/).

10. If you still cannot name the miss, stop. Use the [paddles catalog](/gear/paddles) and compare the live Amazon.com SKUs before you buy.
