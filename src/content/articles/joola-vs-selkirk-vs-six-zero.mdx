## JOOLA in one paragraph

JOOLA models in our catalog — Perseus, Hyperion CAS/C2, Vision — skew toward competitive shapes, carbon surfaces, and players who already hit with intent. Great when you want a modern all-court or power identity.

## Selkirk in one paragraph

Selkirk covers a wide ladder: SLK Halo Control XL for value forgiveness, LUXX Control Air Epic for premium soft game, and VANGUARD Power Air Invikta for elongated power. Ideal if you want a clear control-to-power path inside one brand family.

## Six Zero in one paragraph

Six Zero’s Double Black Diamond Control and Ruby speak to players hunting grit and thermoformed modern construction. If spin and dwell are your love language, start here — after you can already dink without popping everything up.

## How to decide

Pick the brand that sells the model matching your job, not the brand with the loudest sponsorship reel. Then validate weight and grip on court.
