## Pick a job before a brand

### 1. Driveway / tryout paddle
Goal: cheap contact with a ball. Wood or basic fiberglass is enough. Do not overbuy before you know you will play weekly.

**Catalog cue:** Franklin Activator-style wood sets and ultra-budget fiberglass singles.

### 2. Weekly club paddle
Goal: a sweet spot that survives mixed-skill round robins. Midweight, comfortable grip, boring reliability.

**Catalog cues:** niupipo fiberglass, Selkirk SLK Helix Pro, Franklin Signature Series value frames.

### 3. Value carbon / thermoformed step-up
Goal: a more modern face without jumping to $$$$$ flagships. Useful when your contact is already consistent and you want more bite or pop.

**Catalog cues:** Roycelong-style T700 16mm frames, Pickln Piknix Pro thermoformed options, Selkirk SLK Latitude / Nexus in the upper budget band.

## Specs that still matter under $100

Ignore influencer unboxings. Check:

1. **Weight comfort** — if your elbow complains, go lighter before chasing power.
2. **Grip size** — add an [overgrip](/guides/pickleball-accessories-buying-guide) before you blame the paddle.
3. **Shape** — widebody/control shapes forgive; elongated shapes add reach and miss more off-center.
4. **Core thickness** — ~16mm often feels more controlled; thinner cores can feel poppier.
5. **Return window** — online buyers need a store that lets you send back a lemon.

Deeper framework: [How to choose a pickleball paddle](/guides/how-to-choose-a-pickleball-paddle). Materials primer: [carbon vs fiberglass](/guides/carbon-fiber-vs-fiberglass-paddles).

## What not to expect

- **Pro-level spin grit that lasts forever** — budget faces vary; grit wears.
- **One paddle that beats every $250 frame** — marketing will claim otherwise.
- **Shoes optional** — court shoes protect joints more than a $20 paddle upgrade will raise your rating. See [best pickleball shoes](/guides/best-pickleball-shoes-2026).

## When to leave the under-$100 band

Move up when **two** of these are true:

- You play 2+ times per week and know you want control *or* power *or* spin on purpose.
- You can describe what your current paddle does poorly (launches long, dead sweet spot, no bite).
- You have proper court shoes and a return-friendly seller lined up.

Then use [Best paddles 2026](/guides/best-pickleball-paddles-2026) and the control / power / spin guides — not a random “#1 paddle” listicle.

## Buying checklist

1. Name the job (tryout / weekly / value upgrade).
2. Stay in $–$$$ unless you have a clear reason.
3. Shortlist two models max.
4. Confirm exact SKU, price, and returns on the store.
5. Re-evaluate after five sessions — not one honeymoon rally.
