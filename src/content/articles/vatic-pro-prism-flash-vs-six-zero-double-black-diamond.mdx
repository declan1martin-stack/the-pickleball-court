## Who should buy which paddle

You belong on this page if you already keep the ball in and you are choosing among these four catalog lines. Players shopping this matchup are usually past a first paddle — often a 3.5 game and up — and they want a control upgrade they can explain. The catalog skill line is **advanced** on Prism Flash, Double Black Diamond Control, and Vatic Flash. Ruby’s description is **intermediate** players stepping into carbon-face performance. “3.5” is the search audience, not a rating printed on any SKU.

- Buy **Prism Flash** if the miss is touch: resets, drops, and a softer cold-molded meet, in the **$$$$** band, with a cover in the box.
- Buy **DBD Control** if the miss is grit and spin longevity, plus foam the listing ties to blocks, and you care about dwell on windy outdoor drops. Budget the **$$$$$** band. Read the thickness in the cart. It is not printed here.

- Buy **Ruby 16mm** if you want Six Zero’s printed thermoformed blend of pop and dwell in a hybrid shape for all-court mixed doubles. Same **$$$$$** band. It is not the grit listing.

**Kitchen resets.** Prism Flash is the paddle whose build word is control/touch. That is the reset and drop job in the **$$$$** band. DBD Control can still be the drop paddle when the sentence you need is dwell on a windy outdoor third-shot, not a colder, softer mold. Those are different drop stories. Flash 16mm does not print a reset sentence. Ruby prints pop **and** dwell, which is a blend, not a soft-hands specialist. Wider control shopping: [best paddles for control](/guides/best-pickleball-paddles-for-control).

**Blocks and counters.** Only DBD Control ties foam to blocks: the walls **dampen vibration on blocks**. That is a manufacturer line, not a vibration score and not an arm-care claim. Prism, Flash, and Ruby do not print a block sentence. Do not promote foam-injected walls on the Vatic paddles into the DBD block line.

**Put-away pop.** Flash 16mm is the power-and-spin sentence, with a hybrid power shape. Ruby is the thermoformed pop-and-dwell sentence. Prism and DBD are not put-away listings. If the only miss is drive speed, leave this page for [best paddles for power](/guides/best-pickleball-paddles-for-power).

**Sweet spot.** None of the four print a width, a length, or a sweet-spot measurement. Ruby’s **hybrid shape bridging elongated reach and widebody forgiveness** is the only geometry line. The Ruby description says the paddle “hits the sweet spot” for players who want thermoformed power with manageable weight. Read that as fit, not as a measured zone. Shape context: [elongated vs widebody](/guides/elongated-vs-widebody-pickleball-paddles).

Foam is perimeter construction on Prism, DBD, and Flash. Ruby does not print foam walls. How to read foam without turning it into a power rating: [foam cores explained](/guides/pickleball-paddle-foam-cores-explained). Thickness, when it is printed, is 16mm — and DBD does not print it. ## Buying checklist

1. Name the miss in plain language: soft reset and drop, grit plus damped blocks, hybrid power shape, or thermoformed pop and dwell.
2. Require the **exact catalog sentence**. Cold-molded control/touch is Prism Flash. Raw T700 grit and spin longevity, plus foam that dampens vibration on blocks, is DBD Control. Hybrid power shape and a carbon-encased edge are Vatic Flash. 16mm thermoformed pop and dwell, plus a hybrid shape, is Ruby.
3. Read the band, then Amazon.com. Prism Flash is **$$$$**. DBD Control, Flash 16mm, and Ruby 16mm are **$$$$$**. A “value price” sentence on Flash does not change that badge.
4. Read thickness only where it is printed. Prism and Vatic Flash print a **16mm poly core**. Ruby prints **16mm** on the thermoformed line. DBD Control prints **no millimeters**. Confirm 16mm versus 14mm in the cart on every SKU.
5. Do not invent ounces, a sweet-spot size, a grit score, or a spin RPM. None of the four print them. Ruby’s manageable weight is not an ounce.
6. Do not invent aramid, and do not invent thermoformed on DBD Control or on Vatic Flash. Only Ruby prints thermoformed. Only Prism prints cold-molded.
7. Approval: Prism Flash, DBD Control, and Ruby do not print an approval line. Vatic Flash prints **USAPA approved**. For a sanctioned match, confirm the exact model on the [USA Pickleball approved equipment list](https://equipment.usapickleball.org/).
8. A cover ships with Prism Flash only. It does not make that paddle the right feel.
9. If you still cannot name the miss, stop. Use [how to choose a paddle](/guides/how-to-choose-a-pickleball-paddle) and the [paddles catalog](/gear/paddles).
