## Paddle priorities

Forgiveness over flagship power. Halo Control XL and Vision C15 are sensible catalog starting points.

## Shoe priorities

Court shoes that cushion and stabilize — Skechers Viper Court or ASICS Gel-Dedicate — beat running shoes every day. On the [shoes catalog](/gear/shoes), use the **Comfort & Support** filters (wide fit, arch support, extra cushioning, low-impact) to shortlist options that already document those features — useful for joint-friendly play without relying on age-based labels.

## Extra practical notes for US players

Club booking culture, shared carts, and mixed-skill round robins mean your gear has to work for imperfect warmups. Arrive with shoes that do not need a three-week break-in and a paddle you can dink with before your shoulder is hot. If you travel between states for tournaments, keep a spare overgrip and verify your portable net’s packed dimensions fit your trunk without crushing the frame.

When friends ask for a single recommendation, answer with a question: what problem are you solving? Elbow fatigue, missing drops, slipping on dust, or hosting driveway games each point to different catalog picks. That question is the difference between a useful affiliate site and a keyword farm.
