## What the listing actually claims

Retail copy is not a lab sheet. Read the line that is actually on the SKU:

- **Hole count / outdoor job** — Franklin listing: **40 machine-drilled holes** for spin and a balanced flight path. ONIX Fuse G2 listing: **precision-drilled holes** and a balance claim. Dura Fast 40 is named as an outdoor Fast **40** ball in this catalog; the truncated feature line does not print a hole count, so do not invent one. These SKUs are outdoor balls, not indoor ~26-hole gym balls.
- **Construction language** — Franklin: **seamless one-piece** for concrete, asphalt, and other outdoor surfaces. Dura: **rotationally molded**. ONIX Fuse G2: **seam welding** that resists splitting. Do not flatten those three phrases into one ball.
- **Approval / “official” language** — Franklin listing: USAP-approved and official-ball language for the US Open and USA Pickleball. Dura listing: official-ball language for the US Open and tournament approval (USAPA/IFP). Those are **listing bullets**, not proof the event you entered this weekend still uses that SKU. Confirm the live model on the [USA Pickleball approved equipment list](https://equipment.usapickleball.org/).
- **Pack size** — this catalog’s outdoor SKUs are a **12-pack** (Franklin X-40), a **6-pack** (Dura Fast 40), and a **3-pack** (ONIX Fuse G2). Confirm the count in the cart. We do not list other pack sizes, so do not invent a dozen Dura or a 100-count ONIX here.

## Buying checklist

1. Name the surface: **outdoor hard court** (this page) or **gym indoor** ([balls buying guide](/guides/pickleball-balls-buying-guide)). Do not mix them.
2. If the club, league, or event named a ball, buy **that** SKU.
3. If nobody named one, default **Franklin X-40 12-pack**.
4. Shortlist **one** outdoor pack from the tables above. Dura Fast 40 if you want this catalog’s tournament-style 6-pack; ONIX Fuse G2 if you want the outdoor 3-pack.
5. Confirm exact SKU, pack count, seller, approval language, and price on the store — then stop shopping.
