## How to choose

- **Radical Pro (2024)** if you want spin and balanced power at the lowest band, with a 15mm core.
- **Radical Elite** if you want a large sweet spot and can accept less spin.

## Radical Pro (2024)

**Radical Pro (2024)** is for intermediate players who want an all-court paddle: spin plus balanced power. It is the lowest band in the line, **$$**.

Three details back that up. The face is composite/fiberglass with **SpinOn**. The core is a **15mm** control-oriented build. The listed weight is **about 8.1 oz**. It also uses a Dynamic Power Shape and a Comfort Grip System.

## Radical Elite

**Radical Elite** is for a newer intermediate who wants a forgiving, powerful paddle and a large sweet spot. The band is **$$$**, one step above the Pro.

The face is **fiberglass**, and HEAD describes it as smoother, with less spin. The core is an **OTC honeycomb polymer**. The shape is **Dynamic Power Shape**, with that large sweet spot, on a comfort grip.

<ProductSpotlight productId="paddle-head-radical-elite" eyebrow="Newer intermediate — large sweet spot, less spin, $$$" />

## Radical Tour XL

**Tour XL** is for advanced players who want reach and power in a longer frame. The band is **$$$$**.

The shape is **elongated**, for extra reach. The face is **carbon graphite** with Extreme Spin texture. The listed weight is **about 7.6 oz**, on an OTC core. Once length is the only open question, compare shapes on [elongated vs widebody](/guides/elongated-vs-widebody-pickleball-paddles). ## Radical Tour GR

**Tour GR** is for advanced players who want power with more stability and control. It sits in the same **$$$$** band as Tour XL.

**Control Stabilizer Technology** is the stability feature. The core is a **thicker OTC honeycomb**. The face is graphite with Extreme Spin, at a listed **about 7.9 oz**.
