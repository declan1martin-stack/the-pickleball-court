## Court and pacing

A regulation pickleball court is **20×44 feet** with a **7-foot** kitchen on each side ([USA Pickleball](https://usapickleball.org/construction/)), versus a tennis singles court at **27×78 feet**. Points can be shorter, and the non-volley zone creates a tactical layer tennis does not share.

## Gear differences

Solid paddles vs strung racquets; lower launch point on serves; shoes still need lateral court design.

## Skills that transfer

Anticipation, footwork discipline, and competitive temperament transfer. Grip firmness and swing length usually need shrinking.

## Extra practical notes for US players

Club booking culture, shared carts, and mixed-skill round robins mean your gear has to work for imperfect warmups. Arrive with shoes that do not need a three-week break-in and a paddle you can dink with before your shoulder is hot. If you travel between states for tournaments, keep a spare overgrip and verify your portable net’s packed dimensions fit your trunk without crushing the frame.
