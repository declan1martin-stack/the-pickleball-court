If you want cushioning and an easier break-in for social and intermediate play, Skechers Viper Court / Pro are the default shortlist.

## Stability lane: ASICS

If you load the edges hard in competitive doubles, ASICS Gel-Resolution 9 is the premium option; Gel-Dedicate 8 keeps ASICS DNA at a lower tier.

Skechers often wins comfort-first club nights. ASICS often wins when you prioritize structured lateral support. Your foot shape decides the final call.
