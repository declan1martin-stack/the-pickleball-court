## Comfort lane: Skechers

If you want cushioning and an easier break-in for social and intermediate play, Skechers Viper Court / Pro are the default shortlist.

## Stability lane: ASICS

If you load the edges hard in competitive doubles, ASICS Gel-Resolution 9 is the premium option; Gel-Dedicate 8 keeps ASICS DNA at a lower tier.

## The honest verdict

Skechers often wins comfort-first club nights. ASICS often wins when you prioritize structured lateral support. Your foot shape decides the final call.

## Extra practical notes for US players

Club booking culture, shared carts, and mixed-skill round robins mean your gear has to work for imperfect warmups. Arrive with shoes that do not need a three-week break-in and a paddle you can dink with before your shoulder is hot. If you travel between states for tournaments, keep a spare overgrip and verify your portable net’s packed dimensions fit your trunk without crushing the frame.
