## What actually matters in pickleball apparel

- **Freedom of movement** — lateral lunges and quick direction changes mean tight, restrictive fabric gets uncomfortable fast.
- **Moisture-wicking material** — helps with sweat management during longer sessions.
- **Sun protection** — outdoor courts in summer mean real UV exposure over a 2–3 hour session; sleeves and light layers help more than most players expect.
- **Pockets** — a small but real quality-of-life factor for carrying a spare ball between points.

## Buying tips

- **Layer for US shoulder seasons.** Arm sleeves under short sleeves handle the temperature swing between an early morning start and a warm afternoon better than switching outfits mid-day.
- **Avoid all-dark colors for outdoor summer play** — lighter fabrics reflect heat and stay more comfortable over a long session.
- **Check the return policy before buying multiples of a new fit** — sizing varies more across activewear brands than most people expect.
