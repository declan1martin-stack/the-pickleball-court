## What “advanced” means here

We do **not** use DUPR, UTR, or years played. [How we define skill tiers](/guides/how-we-define-skill-tiers) is the dictionary:

- **Beginner / improver** — rallies break down; you are still building a third-shot habit.
- **Intermediate** — you choose pace on purpose and mixed-skill games no longer melt you down. Control-hybrid ~16mm first.
- **Advanced** — you construct points. You punish high balls, reset under pressure, and already have a one-handed or two-handed backhand identity. Gear preference is specific (shape, grit, construction). Flagships make sense here; they rarely fix fundamentals.

Buy for the week you actually play. A $$$$$ paddle can still be the wrong advanced buy if you cannot name the miss.

## Best for control / soft-game advanced

Choose this camp if **kitchen construction is the job**: dinks, drops, resets, and absorbing pace when opponents attack your feet. You want dwell and a stable block, not trampoline pop.

Catalog anchors (confirm the live SKU):

- **Selkirk LUXX Control Air Epic** — listing prints a **Control Air core** for soft hands and kitchen dinks, plus an **Epic widebody** for off-centre contact. Catalog: placement and patience; premium edge-guard build for club and league play. Upper band ($$$$$). This is the premium control flagship — not a beginner XL and not a Perseus twin.
- **Six Zero Double Black Diamond Control** — **raw T700 carbon face** plus **control-tuned foam edge walls** that the listing says dampen vibration on blocks. Catalog: highly searched among advanced players; spin-focused dinks and third-shot dwell. Upper band ($$$$$). Control plus grit — not a power mould.
- **Vatic Pro Prism Flash 16mm** — **T700 raw carbon**, **16mm poly core**, **foam-injected edge walls**, **cold-molded for control/touch**. Catalog skill level: **advanced**. Value band ($$$$) versus flagship Selkirk / Six Zero. Includes cover; listing uses approved-equipment language — confirm the live model.
- **Selkirk SLK Halo Control XL** — **Halo polymer core** listed for **control-first intermediate players**, plus an **XL face** for wide-body blocking. Upper band ($$$$$). Honest here only if you already play advanced *and* still want a larger target without LUXX money. It is **not** the first advanced identity buy if you just left fiberglass — that is the [intermediate](/guides/best-pickleball-paddles-for-intermediate-players) default.

## Best for spin / all-court hybrid

Choose this camp if **mixed doubles is still the job** and you already brush the ball: roll volleys, dipping thirds, shape instead of flat put-aways. You want bite without giving up a usable kitchen.

Catalog anchors (confirm the live SKU):

- **Six Zero Ruby 16mm** — listing prints **16mm thermoformed construction** and a **hybrid shape** (reach plus widebody forgiveness). Catalog: all-court mixed doubles; also the overall intermediate 16mm hybrid. Upper band ($$$$$). Advanced players keep it when they want one paddle for club nights — not a kitchen specialist and not a Perseus-class power frame.
- **JOOLA Hyperion CAS 16** — **Carbon Abrasion Surface (CAS)** listed for high-RPM topspin drives; **elongated handle** aimed at two-handed backhands. Upper band ($$$$$). Spin identity with extra handle — confirm the CAS 16 listing, not a random Hyperion graphic.
- **JOOLA Scorpeus 3S** — **wide-body 3S shape** with a larger sweet spot for blocks and counters; **raw carbon face** for roll volleys and topspin drives. Catalog: doubles players who live at the kitchen. Upper band ($$$$$). Spin in a more forgiving face than an elongated Perseus.
- **Holbrook Mav Pro 2.0** — **T700 3K raw carbon face**, **16mm dual-reactive polypropylene honeycomb**. Catalog: a power/control hybrid for *intermediates* stepping into thermoformed carbon **without jumping to flagship JOOLA pricing**. Upper band ($$$$$). Honest here as the all-court carbon hybrid under JOOLA money — not a Perseus twin and not a “pro series” skill badge.
- **Babolat RBEL** — **graphite skin**, **RPM Max Grit** (listing: max approved texture), **14mm, ~7.6 oz**. Catalog skill level: **advanced**; fast hands and heavy spin. Value band ($$$). This is a **printed light-end 14mm** — not the 16mm default. Use it when hand speed is the named miss, then read [14mm vs 16mm](/guides/14mm-vs-16mm-pickleball-paddles).

## Best for reach / elongated advanced

Choose this camp if **length is the job**: singles, aggressive doubles, two-handed backhands, or balls you currently miss by an inch. You trade a smaller effective sweet spot for extra face (and often handle) length.

Skip elongated if you still mistime resets. Length will not teach contact. Shape explainer: [elongated vs widebody](/guides/elongated-vs-widebody-pickleball-paddles).

Catalog anchors (confirm the live SKU):

- **HEAD Radical Tour XL** — **carbon graphite** hitting surface, **elongated power shape (extra reach)**, Extreme Spin texture, printed **`~7.6 oz`**. Catalog skill level: **advanced**. $$$$ band. One of the few elongated listings here with a **printed ounce line** — still a conventional light-to-mid static number, not a heavy plow-through SKU.
- **JOOLA Ben Johns Hyperion C2 16mm** — listing calls out a Hyperion mould with an **elongated sweet spot** and a Charged Carbon face. Name line includes **16mm**. Upper band ($$$$$). All-court reach without pretending it is a beginner block paddle. The 2026 hub’s all-court compromise.
- **Selkirk VANGUARD Power Air Invikta** — same Invikta elongated power listing as the put-away camp. Use it here when **reach plus pop** is the named miss, not when you only wanted a longer Halo.

## Accessories that support the upgrade

Do not pad the cart. These three SKUs only help **after** the paddle identity is right.

- **Tourna Mega Tac** — extra-tacky overgrip for humidity and long sessions. Three grips per pack. Replace when tack dies; a $$$$$ face with a slick factory wrap is still a bad night.
- **Weighted tungsten tape** — 60-inch roll for custom static mass. This catalog has **no listed SKU in the heavy static-weight band**; adding tungsten to a midweight you already center is the honest plow-through path. Not a substitute for choosing power vs control.
- **Professor Pickleball edge-guard tape** — sizes for **12mm–14mm** and **16mm+** edges. Cheap insurance against court scrapes on a flagship you intend to keep.

## Buying checklist

1. Confirm you are **advanced on this site** — you construct points and can name the miss — via [skill tiers](/guides/how-we-define-skill-tiers). If not, stay on the [intermediate](/guides/best-pickleball-paddles-for-intermediate-players) shortlist.
2. Name **one** job: kitchen/control, put-away/power, spin/all-court, or elongated reach.
3. Shortlist **one** paddle from that camp in the tables above. Do not checkout a family page that offers multiple Pro IV shapes without picking (JOOLA Pro IV).
4. Match leftover forks after the paddle: [weight](/guides/lightweight-vs-midweight-vs-heavy-pickleball-paddles), [thickness](/guides/14mm-vs-16mm-pickleball-paddles), [shape](/guides/elongated-vs-widebody-pickleball-paddles), [construction](/guides/thermoformed-vs-traditional-pickleball-paddles).
5. Add Tourna Mega Tac if the factory wrap is already slick; add tungsten only after you like the unweighted paddle; add edge-guard tape if you scrape courts.
6. Confirm exact SKU, seller, approval language, and **current store price** — then stop shopping. $–$$$$$ badges are not live prices.
