## XPLR

**XPLR** is for beginners who want a light, forgiving paddle from a known tennis brand. It is the higher band of the two, **$$$$**.

The face is a **fiberglass composite** skin. The core is **polypropylene honeycomb**, at **13mm**, with a listed weight of **about 7.8 oz**. The same feature line calls the swing lightweight and easy, and the frame includes a shock-resistant bumper.

## RBEL

**RBEL** is for players who want fast hands and heavy spin. The catalog skill line is advanced. The band is **$$$**, one step below the XPLR.

The face is a **graphite skin** with **RPM Max Grit**. The listing describes that grit as max USA Pickleball texture. The core is an **EVA-enclosed polypropylene honeycomb**, at **14mm** and **about 7.6 oz**. The feature line calls the paddle light and maneuverable.

1. Name the job: a forgiving beginner paddle, or fast hands and spin.
2. Match the band. XPLR is **$$$$**. RBEL is **$$$**.
3. Open the listing and match the model to the one you picked.
4. For a sanctioned match, check the exact model on the [USA Pickleball approved equipment list](https://equipment.usapickleball.org/). Neither listing here says the paddle is approved. The RBEL grit line is about texture.
