This is the **upgrade shortlist** after wood, a toy set, or first fiberglass — not a flagship thermoformed power page. Intermediate players on this site need a real all-court paddle: **midweight, about 16mm, control-hybrid first**. Pick the job, then shortlist from the [paddles catalog](/gear/paddles).

If you still spray kitchen contact, stay on the [beginner paddles](/guides/best-pickleball-paddles-for-beginners) ladder. If you already construct points and want a specific identity, use [control](/guides/best-pickleball-paddles-for-control), [power](/guides/best-pickleball-paddles-for-power), or [spin](/guides/best-pickleball-paddles-for-spin). Spec forks (weight, thickness, construction, shape) live in the nearby-decision section below.

## What “intermediate” means here

We do **not** use DUPR, UTR, or years played. [How we define skill tiers](/guides/how-we-define-skill-tiers) is the dictionary:

- **Beginner / improver** — rallies break down; you are still building a third-shot habit. Forgiving fiberglass or accessible carbon.
- **Intermediate** — you choose pace on purpose (dink, drop, or drive) and mixed-skill games no longer melt you down. You may enter local round robins. Control vs power identity starts to matter. Thermoformed or raw-carbon options start to matter *if* your contact is consistent.
- **Advanced** — you construct points. Flagships make sense here; they rarely fix fundamentals.

Buy for the week you actually play. A $$$$$ paddle can still be the wrong intermediate buy.

## Best overall intermediate all-court

Choose this camp if **mixed doubles is the job**: you already dink and drive on purpose, and you want one paddle for club nights — not a kitchen specialist and not a banger frame.

Default construction: **~16mm**, **hybrid or balanced shape**, **control-hybrid feel**. Most named-brand 16mm carbon in this catalog does **not** print ounces; treat [midweight as the shopping default](/guides/lightweight-vs-midweight-vs-heavy-pickleball-paddles) and confirm the live listing.

Catalog anchors (confirm the live SKU):

- **Six Zero Ruby 16mm** — listing prints **16mm thermoformed construction** and a **hybrid shape** (reach plus widebody forgiveness). Catalog copy: all-court mixed doubles and intermediate players stepping into carbon-face performance. Upper catalog band ($$$$$). This is the overall 16mm hybrid — not a beginner block paddle and not a Perseus-class power frame.
- **Holbrook Mav Pro 2.0** — **16mm dual-reactive polypropylene honeycomb** with a **T700 3K raw carbon face**. Catalog description: a power/control hybrid for intermediates stepping into thermoformed carbon **without jumping to flagship JOOLA pricing**. Upper band ($$$$$). Confirm the live listing before you treat it as a Perseus twin.
- **HEAD Radical Pro (2024)** — **15mm control-oriented core**, **~8.1 oz**, Dynamic Power Shape, composite/fiberglass with SpinOn. Catalog: versatile all-court, skill level intermediate ($$). The clearest **printed midweight ounce line** in this shortlist. Nearby thickness — 15mm is not 16mm.

## Buying checklist

1. Confirm you are **intermediate on this site** — pace on purpose, mixed games stable — via [skill tiers](/guides/how-we-define-skill-tiers). If not, stay on the beginner ladder.
2. Default **midweight / ~16mm / control-hybrid**. Name one job: all-court, kitchen, value carbon, or honest pop.
3. Shortlist **one** paddle from that camp in the tables above. Do not checkout a family page that offers 14mm and 16mm without picking (Volair Mach 2).
4. Match leftover forks after the paddle: [weight](/guides/lightweight-vs-midweight-vs-heavy-pickleball-paddles), [thickness](/guides/14mm-vs-16mm-pickleball-paddles), [shape](/guides/elongated-vs-widebody-pickleball-paddles), [construction](/guides/thermoformed-vs-traditional-pickleball-paddles).
5. Confirm exact SKU, seller, approval language, and **current store price** — then stop shopping. $–$$$$$ badges are not live prices.
