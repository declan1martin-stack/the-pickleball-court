## Fiberglass feel

Often springier and easier for recreational pop. Helpful when you need help generating ball speed.

## Carbon feel

Usually firmer feedback with more potential bite for topspin and sidespin when you brush correctly.

## Practical recommendation

Beginners: prioritize sweet spot and weight. Intermediates ready to shape the ball: carbon or CAS-style surfaces in our catalog. Advanced spin players: raw carbon options like Double Black Diamond Control.
