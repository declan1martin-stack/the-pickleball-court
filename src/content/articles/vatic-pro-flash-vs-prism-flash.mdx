## Flash

**Flash** is for aggressive players who want power and spin. The band is **$$$$$**.

The face is **T700 heat-textured raw carbon for spin**. The core is a **16mm poly core** with **foam-injected walls**, in a **hybrid power shape**. The feature list also names a **carbon-encased edge**, **USA Pickleball-approved**. It does not say thermoformed. Heat-textured is the face line. What the mold words mean is [thermoformed vs traditional](/guides/thermoformed-vs-traditional-pickleball-paddles).

## Prism Flash

**Prism Flash** is for control-oriented players who want premium carbon feel. The band is **$$$$**.

The face is a **T700 raw carbon face**. The core is a **16mm poly core** with **foam-injected edge walls**. The build line says **cold-molded for control/touch**, and it **includes a cover**. It does not say thermoformed, and it does not list an approval line.
