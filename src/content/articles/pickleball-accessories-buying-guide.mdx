## The core four

<ComparisonTable productIds={["acc-franklin-overgrip","acc-tourna-mega-tac","acc-edge-guard","acc-tungsten-tape"]} />

**Overgrips (Franklin)**
Your factory grip degrades with sweat and use, and a slick grip directly costs you control on fast exchanges at the kitchen line. Most players should replace overgrips every few weeks of regular play — it's the single cheapest performance upgrade available.

**Extra-tacky overgrips (Tourna Mega Tac)**
For players who sweat heavily or play in humid conditions, a tacky overgrip like Tourna Mega Tac restores feel between full grip replacements and helps during sticky summer sessions.

**Edge guard tape**
Protects the vulnerable edge of the paddle face from chips and cracks caused by court contact — cheap insurance for a paddle you've already invested in, especially if you scrape low volleys.

**Tungsten tape**
Applied to the paddle's edge or handle, tungsten tape lets you fine-tune weight and swing balance without buying a new paddle — a favorite among more advanced players dialing in feel. Beginners generally don't need it yet.

## Buying tips

- **Replace overgrips proactively, not reactively.** Waiting until a grip feels obviously slick means you've already been playing at reduced control for a while.
- **Edge guard tape is cheap insurance.** If you already dropped money on a mid-tier or premium paddle, a few dollars of tape protecting the edge is an easy call.
- **Tungsten tape is a fine-tuning tool, not a fix.** It adjusts an already-good paddle's feel; it won't turn a beginner paddle into an advanced one.
