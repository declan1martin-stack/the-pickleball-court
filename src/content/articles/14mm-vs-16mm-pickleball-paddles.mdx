## What the mm line actually means

**Core thickness** is the honeycomb (or listed core) depth, not a skill rating and not a synonym for “power paddle.” Catalog copy is not standardized:

- **16mm** — Perseus, Ruby, Holbrook Mav Pro 2.0, Vatic Prism Flash, Piknix Pro. Hyperion C2 and Franklin McGrath use **16mm in the listing name**.
- **14mm** — Babolat RBEL is the catalog’s explicit 14mm line (`14mm, ~7.6 oz`).
- **Nearby thinner SKUs** — 13mm Latitude / Franklin Signature Pro / Babolat XPLR; 15mm Vision C15; 12mm JOOLA Essentials Performance.
- **Both on one listing** — Volair Mach 2 copy says USAPA approved with **14mm and 16mm options**. Confirm which thickness you are adding to cart.

Do not flatten 12mm fiberglass and 14mm graphite into one paddle. A Vision C15 15mm carbon is not a Perseus-class 16mm, and neither is a 13mm Franklin.

## Buy 16mm when you want dwell and a planted kitchen

Choose **16mm** if **control of first contact is the job**: drops, resets, blocks, mixed-skill doubles, and a first upgrade from wood or cheap fiberglass. You are trading some trampoline pop for extra dwell. That is the default in [how to choose a paddle](/guides/how-to-choose-a-pickleball-paddle) for outdoor play, where the ball can skid.

Skip 16mm as a *requirement* if you already center the ball, want a lighter swing, and the current paddle feels dead on put-aways. Thickness will not teach a two-handed backhand — that is [shape](/guides/elongated-vs-widebody-pickleball-paddles).

Catalog anchors with an **explicit 16mm line** (confirm the live SKU):

- **Vatic Pro Prism Flash 16mm** — T700 raw carbon, **16mm poly core**, foam-injected edge walls, **cold-molded for control/touch**. The honest 16mm carbon default when you want dwell without a thermoformed unibody story ($$$$).
- **JOOLA Perseus 16mm** — **16mm thermoformed edge** for pop without giving up touch resets. Power-oriented Perseus-family frame for players who already drive with intent. Upper catalog band ($$$$$). Cross-check [best paddles for power](/guides/best-pickleball-paddles-for-power).
- **Six Zero Ruby 16mm** — **16mm thermoformed construction** listed as a hybrid shape (reach plus forgiveness). Intermediate carbon step-up — not a beginner block paddle.
- **Holbrook Mav Pro 2.0** — **16mm dual-reactive polypropylene honeycomb** with a T700 3K raw carbon face. Catalog description positions it as a thermoformed-carbon step-up under flagship JOOLA pricing ($$$$$). Confirm the live listing before you treat it as a Perseus twin.

## Buy thinner (including 14mm) when you want pop and hand speed

Choose **thinner** if **pop and a lighter swing are the job** and you already hit the center. Shoppers type “14mm vs 16mm”; this catalog’s thinner camp is the SKUs that actually print 12mm, 13mm, 14mm, or 15mm. You give up some dwell. You gain a trampoline-like response that punishes late drops if your contact is still messy.

This is **not** the default for new players. It is the honest pick if your current 16mm feels muted on drives and your kitchen is already reliable.

Catalog anchors with an **explicit thinner mm line** (confirm the live SKU):

- **Babolat RBEL** — the catalog’s explicit **14mm** (`14mm, ~7.6 oz`), graphite skin, RPM Max Grit, EVA-enclosed polypropylene honeycomb. Light/maneuverable listing aimed at fast hands and spin ($$$). This is the 14mm camp, not a 16mm control hybrid.
- **Selkirk SLK Latitude 13mm** — **13mm SX4 polymer core** for lighter, poppier swings; Graphite SpinFlex face. $$$ band. Treat it as a developing-player 13mm, not a 14mm Babolat twin.
- **Franklin Signature Series Pro 13mm** — **13mm polypropylene core**, elongated 16.5" shape, MaxGrit fiberglass. Lower catalog band ($$). Shape plus thickness — do not flatten it into “just a 14mm.”
- **Babolat XPLR** — **13mm, ~7.8 oz**, fiberglass composite, polypropylene honeycomb. Beginner-friendly light swing ($$$$). 13mm is not 14mm.
- **JOOLA Vision C15** — **15mm profile** that adds pop for recreational drive-and-volley games; accessible carbon face. Entry-premium JOOLA search ($$$$$). Middle thickness — closer to the thinner camp than to a 16mm Perseus.
- **JOOLA Essentials Performance** — **12mm core**, reinforced fiberglass, Response polypropylene honeycomb. Named-brand traditional step-up in the entry band ($). Thinnest core in this shortlist — a first paddle, not a tournament 14mm.

## Nearby labels that are not this decision

Shoppers mash three different stories into one cart. Keep them separate:

- **Shape** — elongated vs widebody changes how you miss, not how thick the core is. Franklin Signature Pro is both elongated **and** 13mm. Perseus is a power-oriented 16mm. Solve thickness here, then [shape](/guides/elongated-vs-widebody-pickleball-paddles).
- **Construction** — thermoformed vs cold-molded is the mold, not the mm. Ruby and Perseus are 16mm *and* thermoformed; Vatic Prism Flash is 16mm *and* cold-molded. See [thermoformed vs traditional](/guides/thermoformed-vs-traditional-pickleball-paddles).
- **Power vs control shortlists** — those guides sort by job. A 16mm Perseus can be the power pick; a 16mm Vatic can be the control pick. Thickness is the filter, not the whole answer.

## Buying checklist

1. Name the job: **dwell / kitchen** (16mm) or **pop / lighter swing** (thinner, including 14mm). Do not name a pro.
2. If you cannot describe your mishits yet, default **16mm**.
3. Read the listing for the actual mm. “Power” and “control” are not enough. If the family offers 14mm and 16mm, pick one SKU.
4. Shortlist **one** paddle from that camp in the tables above. Match [shape](/guides/elongated-vs-widebody-pickleball-paddles) and [construction](/guides/thermoformed-vs-traditional-pickleball-paddles) after thickness.
5. Confirm exact SKU, seller, approval language, and price on the store — then stop shopping.

A 14mm paddle will not fix late preparation. A 16mm will not give you unibody pop. Buy the thickness you already play, not the one a highlight reel uses.
</Callout>
