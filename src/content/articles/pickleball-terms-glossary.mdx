## Kitchen (NVZ)

Non-volley zone. The painted area that forces soft-game craft.

## Dink

The chess move of pickleball. Soft, low, patient.

## Third-shot drop

The bridge from baseline to kitchen for the serving team.

## Speed-up

A sudden attack when a ball sits up — high risk, high reward.

## Erne & ATP

Highlight-reel shots. Learn resets before you learn circus shots.

## Banger

Affectionate (sometimes not) term for a hard-hitting pace player.
