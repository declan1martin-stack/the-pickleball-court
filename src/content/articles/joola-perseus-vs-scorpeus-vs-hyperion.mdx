## Buying checklist

1. Name the miss: elongated power, thermoformed put-away, wide-body kitchen blocks, elongated sweet spot, two-handed handle, placement, or a first fiberglass paddle.
2. Require the **exact catalog sentence**. Elongated Perseus is 3S and Pro IV. Wide-body is Scorpeus 3S. Elongated sweet spot is Hyperion C2. Elongated two-handed handle is CAS 16. Thermoformed edge is Perseus 16mm only.
3. Do not paste **16.5 by 7.5**, **16 by 8**, or **Aero-Curve** onto these listings. This catalog does not print them here.
4. Read thickness only where it is printed. Pro IV and Perseus 16mm print **16mm**. Vision prints **15mm**. Essentials prints **12mm**. Perseus 3S, Scorpeus 3S, and both Hyperion feature lists do not print a core millimeter. C2’s name includes 16mm. Confirm the cart.
5. Do not invent ounces, a sweet-spot size, or a spin RPM. CAS 16’s high-RPM line and balanced swing weight are not lab numbers.
6. Read the band, then Amazon.com. Essentials is **$**. The other eight paddles on this page are **$$$$$**. Vision’s flagship-pricing sentence does not move the badge.
7. Approval language is not interchangeable. Perseus 16mm prints a **USAPA-approved shape**. Essentials prints **USAPA approved**. Pro IV prints **UPA-A + USAP certified**. Scorpeus prints **UPA-A certified**. Agassi Graf prints **USAP and UPA-A certified**. Perseus 3S, both Hyperions, and Vision do not print an approval line. For a sanctioned match, confirm the exact model on the [USA Pickleball approved equipment list](https://equipment.usapickleball.org/).
8. On Pro IV, confirm the shape. The listing also offers other Pro IV shapes.
9. If you still cannot name the miss, stop. Use [how to choose a paddle](/guides/how-to-choose-a-pickleball-paddle), the [paddles catalog](/gear/paddles), and [best pickleball gifts 2026](/guides/best-pickleball-gifts-2026).
