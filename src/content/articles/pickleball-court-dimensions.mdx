## Dimensions that matter

According to [USA Pickleball court construction guidance](https://usapickleball.org/construction/) and the [Official USA Pickleball Rulebook](https://usapickleball.org/rules/), a regulation pickleball court is **20 feet wide by 44 feet long**. The non-volley zone (kitchen) is **7 feet deep by 20 feet wide** on each side of the net. Net height is **36 inches at the posts / sidelines** and **34 inches at center**.

Measure twice before you paint or tape permanently. USA Pickleball also notes that a total playing surface of about **30×60 feet** is the recommended minimum around those lines (34×64 preferred when space allows).

## Portable setup sequence

Clear debris, assemble the frame per manufacturer instructions, tension the center strap to **34 inches**, verify posts at **36 inches**, then mark baselines and sidelines to the 20×44 footprint. Re-check center height after the first few games — straps stretch.

## Outdoor court tips

Wind, uneven asphalt, and cold-weather brittle plastic stakes are real. Weight the frame safely and do not leave nets out in storms.
