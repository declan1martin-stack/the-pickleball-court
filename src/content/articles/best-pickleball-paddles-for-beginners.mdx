## What beginners should optimize

Sweet spot size, comfortable grip, midweight feel, and a paddle that does not punish mishits at the kitchen.

## Our beginner shortlist

JOOLA Vision C15 for accessible carbon entry, niupipo for a true budget fiberglass start, Wilson Profile Team for a trusted recreational brand, and Selkirk SLK Halo Control XL when you want a control-first step-up still in the value band — not a flagship $$$ paddle.
