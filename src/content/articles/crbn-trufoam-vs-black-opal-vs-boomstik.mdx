Three foam paddles share the **$$$$$** band. **CRBN TruFoam Barrage** and **Six Zero Black Opal** are the 14mm paddles. **Selkirk LABS Project Boomstik** is the elongated 16mm.

How those cores differ is the [foam-cores explainer](/guides/pickleball-paddle-foam-cores-explained). Every brand is in the [paddles catalog](/gear/paddles).

A TruFoam floating core, G4 aerospace solid foam, and a BoomCore that pairs PureFoam with an EVA power ring are three different builds. The shared $$$$$ badge does not make them one paddle.
</Callout>

## How to choose

- **TruFoam Barrage** for fast hands and pop on a hybrid AeroCurve, at a single 14mm.
- **Black Opal** for the 14mm solid-foam paddle that lists a weight.
- **Project Boomstik** for elongated reach at 16mm. It is for intermediate to advanced players. Those words are defined in [how we define skill tiers](/guides/how-we-define-skill-tiers).

Black Opal is for serious competitors and aspiring pros who want a thinner foam paddle.

## Before you buy

1. Name what you want: kitchen pop on a 14mm hybrid, a 14mm solid-foam paddle with a listed weight, or elongated reach at 16mm.
2. All three are **$$$$$**, above $150. Check the current price on Amazon.com before you buy. The carts can still differ.
3. Match the model on the listing. For the Boomstik, the cart should say elongated and 16mm.
4. For a sanctioned match, check the exact model on the [USA Pickleball approved equipment list](https://equipment.usapickleball.org/).
