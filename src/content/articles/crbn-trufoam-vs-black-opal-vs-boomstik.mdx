Three foam paddles sit in the same **$$$$$** band. Price does not pick one. The core sentence and the shape do.

**CRBN TruFoam Barrage** is the 14mm hybrid for kitchen pop. **Six Zero Black Opal** is the other 14mm, and it is the only one with a printed weight. **Selkirk LABS Project Boomstik** is the elongated 16mm.

Stay on this page when those three names are already the shortlist. How to read foam words is the [foam-cores explainer](/guides/pickleball-paddle-foam-cores-explained). Every brand is in the [paddles catalog](/gear/paddles).

Price badges are catalog bands. **$** is a list price at or under $50, **$$** to $75, **$$$** to $100, **$$$$** to $150, and **$$$$$** above $150.

TruFoam floating core, G4 aerospace solid foam, and a BoomCore that pairs PureFoam with an EVA power ring are different listings. A shared $$$$$ badge does not make them the same paddle.
</Callout>

## How to choose

- **TruFoam Barrage** if you want fast hands and pop on a hybrid AeroCurve, at a single 14mm.
- **Black Opal** if you want the 14mm solid-foam listing that prints a weight.
- **Project Boomstik** if you want elongated reach and a printed 16mm.

Boomstik’s skill line is intermediate to advanced. How this site uses those words is [how we define skill tiers](/guides/how-we-define-skill-tiers). TruFoam’s listing names a kitchen job and does not print a skill line. Black Opal’s description says serious competitors and aspiring pros.

## CRBN TruFoam Barrage 14mm

**TruFoam Barrage** is for players who want fast hands and pop during kitchen exchanges. The band is **$$$$$**.

The shape is a **hybrid AeroCurve**, built at a **single 14mm** thickness, so this row is not a 14mm-and-16mm family page. The core is a **TruFoam floating core** with Toray T700 carbon fiber. The approval line is **USAP approved at PBCoR 43**, and **UPA-A approved**. Ounces and face inches are not printed. PBCoR 43 stays on this row only.

## Six Zero Black Opal 14mm

**Black Opal** is for serious competitors and aspiring pros who want a thinner foam paddle. The band is **$$$$$**, the same band as the TruFoam.

The core is a **14mm G4 aerospace solid foam** core. The face is **Diamond Tough raw carbon**. The average weight is **8.0 to 8.3 ounces**, the only weight on this page. Approval is **USA Pickleball and the UPA**. Face inches and handle length are not printed. The [weight guide](/guides/lightweight-vs-midweight-vs-heavy-pickleball-paddles) places that ounce range. **Project Boomstik** is for aggressive players shopping the elongated 16mm. The catalog skill line is **intermediate to advanced**. The band is **$$$$$**.

The shape is **elongated, 16.5 by 7.45 inches**, with a **5.8-inch handle**. The core is a **16mm BoomCore** that pairs PureFoam with an EVA power ring. The face is **Multistrata T700** with InfiniGrit. Ounces are not printed, and neither is an approval line. This row is the elongated 16mm. Confirm the cart says elongated and 16mm. ## Before you buy

1. Name the job: kitchen pop on a 14mm hybrid, a 14mm solid-foam paddle with a printed weight, or elongated reach at 16mm.
2. Read the band. All three are $$$$$, which means above $150. The carts can still differ.
3. Open the live listing and match the model to the row. On the Boomstik, confirm elongated and 16mm.
4. Keep PBCoR 43 on the TruFoam row, and keep 8.0 to 8.3 ounces on the Black Opal row.
5. For a sanctioned match, check the exact model on the [USA Pickleball approved equipment list](https://equipment.usapickleball.org/).
6. If you still cannot name the job, use the [paddles catalog](/gear/paddles) and [how to choose a paddle](/guides/how-to-choose-a-pickleball-paddle).
