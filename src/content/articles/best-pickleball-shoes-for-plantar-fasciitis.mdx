## Important disclaimer

This is gear guidance, not medical advice. Sharp or persistent pain deserves a clinician, not just a new SKU.

## What to look for

Heel cushioning, secure midfoot, court outsole, and a ride that does not slam your joints on outdoor hard courts.

## Catalog starting points

Skechers Viper Court Pro for comfort-forward court cushioning; ASICS Gel-Dedicate / Gel-Resolution when you want structured court support with Gel cushioning heritage.
