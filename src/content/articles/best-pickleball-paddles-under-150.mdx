## Buying checklist

1. Treat **under $150** as a search. This catalog has **$$$–$$$$** bands, not a dollar cap.
2. Name one job: cold-molded control, value thermoformed, graphite Tour, SpinFlex club, 14mm fast hands, Nomex widebody, or a forgiving rec face.
3. Quote ounces only where printed: Tour GR **~7.9 oz**, Tour XL **~7.6 oz**, RBEL **~7.6 oz**, XPLR **~7.8 oz**. Prism, Piknix, Nexus, Latitude, Z5, Elite, and Wilson do not print ounces.
4. Quote millimeters only where printed: Prism and Piknix **16mm**, Latitude and XPLR **13mm**, RBEL **14mm**. Tour GR, Tour XL, Nexus, Z5, Elite, and Wilson do not print millimeters.
5. Keep approval language on the listing that prints it. Z5 prints **USAPA approved**. Latitude prints **USAPA-ready** inside a performance phrase. RBEL prints **max USAPA texture** on the grit line. Prism Flash and Piknix Pro do not print an approval line.
6. Ignore two-packs and bundles on this page. Open the single SKU.
7. Confirm seller, returns, and today’s price on Amazon.com. Then play five sessions before you judge the face.
