## The point, simply

Serve underhand. After the serve, the return must bounce, and the next shot must bounce too (the two-bounce / double-bounce rule). After that, volleys are allowed — except you cannot volley while touching the non-volley zone (kitchen).

## Scoring basics

Recreational and many club games still use classic side-out scoring: games often play to **11, win by 2**, and **only the serving side scores**. Some leagues and formats use rally scoring instead — ask the host before the first serve. When in doubt for sanctioned play, defer to the current [USA Pickleball rulebook](https://usapickleball.org/rules/).

## Kitchen discipline

The non-volley zone is the **7-foot** area on each side of the net (see [court dimensions](/guides/pickleball-court-dimensions)). Volleying while touching that zone — including the lines — is a fault. Momentum that carries you into the kitchen after a volley still faults if you touch it.

## Extra practical notes for US players

Club booking culture, shared carts, and mixed-skill round robins mean your gear has to work for imperfect warmups. Arrive with shoes that do not need a three-week break-in and a paddle you can dink with before your shoulder is hot. If you travel between states for tournaments, keep a spare overgrip and verify your portable net’s packed dimensions fit your trunk without crushing the frame.
