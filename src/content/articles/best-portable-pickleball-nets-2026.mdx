## What makes a portable net worth buying

Fast setup, regulation dimensions, center strap tension, and a frame that does not collapse when a ball hammers the tape. Per [USA Pickleball](https://usapickleball.org/construction/), that means **36 inches at the posts** and **34 inches at center** on a **20×44-foot** court. Backyard and park players also care about packed size and weather exposure.

## Franklin Signature Pro

A top-searched home-court option for families and HOA driveways. Strong default if you want regulation size without a permanent install.

## ONIX portable system

Favoured when you want premium tension and a travel-ready case for clubs or coaching.

## Rally deluxe

Practical for backyards and multi-use spaces where tool-free setup matters.

## Selkirk premium portable

A natural match if you already trust Selkirk paddles and want a higher-end home court feel.
