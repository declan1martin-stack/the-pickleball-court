## What “regulation” means before you buy

Per [USA Pickleball](https://usapickleball.org/construction/), a regulation court is **20 by 44 feet**, with the net **36 inches at the posts** and **34 inches at center**. A portable frame still has to hold that centre height after you tension the strap and after a few sessions of packing it away.

Printed catalog anchors on this page:

- **Franklin** — regulation-size portable frame; sets up in under 5 minutes; powder-coated steel poles for backyard and driveway courts.
- **Rally** — tool-free telescoping poles; reinforced centre strap for official **34-inch** centre height; cottages and multi-use spaces.

- **Selkirk** — competition-grade netting; powder-coated frame for repeated outdoor US use; premium brand alignment for home-court builders.
