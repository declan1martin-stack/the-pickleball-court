## Fit > brand story

Wide feet need a last that does not crush the fifth metatarsal during lateral loads. Try end-of-day fit checks when feet are slightly swollen.

## Practical shortlist

Start with Skechers Viper Court / Pro for comfort-oriented volume, and compare ASICS models if you need more structured lockdown — confirming width options before you buy.
