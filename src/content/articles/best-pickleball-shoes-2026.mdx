## Why court shoes matter

Pickleball is a lateral sport. Plant, cut, stop at the kitchen, recover for an overhead. Running shoes flex in ways that leave your ankle and knee doing extra work. On outdoor acrylic, durable outsoles also matter because grit eats soft rubber.

## Best comfort-first: Skechers Viper Court line

Viper Court and Viper Court Pro are high-intent searches for players who want court traction without a stiff tennis-shoe break-in. Pro models typically aim at more serious lateral play; the standard Viper Court remains a strong recreational value.

## Best stability-first: ASICS court lineup

Gel-Dedicate 8 is the budget-friendly ASICS on-ramp. Gel-Resolution 9 is the premium lockdown option for competitive players who load the outside edge hard. See [Skechers vs ASICS](/guides/skechers-vs-asics-pickleball).

## Fit checklist

Leave a thumb of toe room, lock the heel, and test a hard lateral step in the store aisle or your hallway before first match use. Wide feet? Read [shoes for wide feet](/guides/best-pickleball-shoes-for-wide-feet).
