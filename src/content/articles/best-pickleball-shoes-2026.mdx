- Leave a thumb of toe room and lock the heel before first match use.
- Test a hard lateral step in the socks you play in.
- Filter the [shoes catalog](/gear/shoes) by Gender and Comfort & Support when the listing documents those tags.
- Foot or knee pain: shoes help comfort; they are not treatment. See [shoes for plantar fasciitis](/guides/best-pickleball-shoes-for-plantar-fasciitis).
