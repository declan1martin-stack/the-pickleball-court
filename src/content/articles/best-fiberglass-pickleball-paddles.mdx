## Who this page is for

You belong here if the next paddle is a **fiberglass or composite face**:

- You are leaving a wood set and want one printed fiberglass single.
- You want a known brand (JOOLA, Franklin, HEAD, Babolat) whose listing still says fiberglass or composite/fiberglass.
- You will quote millimeters and ounces only when that listing prints them.

You do **not** belong here for a raw-carbon flagship. That job is [best raw carbon paddles](/guides/best-raw-carbon-pickleball-paddles). ## What fiberglass means on this page

Fiberglass is a **face material sentence**, not a skill rating.

- **Fiberglass surface / fiberglass face** — Pepkuball, niupipo, JOOLA Essentials, Franklin McGrath (fiberglass surface layer), Franklin Signature Pro (MaxGrit fiberglass), HEAD Radical Elite.
- **Composite / fiberglass** — HEAD Radical Pro (2024) prints a composite/fiberglass surface with SpinOn. Babolat XPLR prints a fiberglass composite skin. Pickle-Ball, Inc. Venom prints a composite face.
- **Carbon + fiberglass hybrid** — Selkirk SLK Helix Pro. Kept as the step-up off this shortlist, not as a pure fiberglass default.

A fiberglass face does not guarantee a large sweet spot, a light swing, or USAPA approval. Only the lines that listing prints do.

## Buying checklist

1. Name **one** job: cheapest covered single, named-brand USAPA starter, printed-ounce budget face, MaxGrit fiberglass, or tennis-brand fiberglass.
2. Require the **face sentence** on the live SKU: fiberglass, fiberglass surface layer, or composite/fiberglass.
3. Quote **mm and oz only when printed**. Pepkuball and Radical Elite print neither. niupipo prints ~7.6 oz. Radical Pro prints 15mm and ~8.1 oz. XPLR prints 13mm and ~7.8 oz. Signature Pro prints 13mm.
4. If you want carbon next, open [carbon fiber vs fiberglass](/guides/carbon-fiber-vs-fiberglass-paddles), then the [budget](/guides/best-budget-pickleball-paddles) or [under $150](/guides/best-pickleball-paddles-under-150) brief.
5. Confirm seller, approval language, and **current store price**. Approximate price tiers are not live prices.
